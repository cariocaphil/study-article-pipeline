# Article Filter Criteria

## Purpose
Define what counts as an acceptable study article for the pipeline.
Apply this when the filter agent assesses candidate URLs.

## Accept
- Genuine critical reviews or analytical essays about the topic
- Opinion pieces that engage substantively with the work
- Interview-based articles that include critical analysis
- Articles from established film/culture/literary publications or blogs
- Minimum 300 words of body text

## Reject
- Plot summaries with no critical analysis
- Ticketing or showtimes pages
- Trailer or clip pages
- Listicles ("10 best films of...")
- Articles primarily in a different language than requested
- Paywalled articles where less than 200 words are accessible
- User reviews or comments sections
- Press releases or promotional copy

## Edge cases
- Syndicated articles (e.g. sapo.pt republishing dn.pt): accept if full text
  is accessible, use the original publication as source_name
- Short reviews under 300 words: accept only if the analysis is substantive
- Mixed-language articles: accept if 80%+ is in the target language
