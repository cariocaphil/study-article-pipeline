# Translation Adequacy Rubric

Use this rubric when judging whether a proposed translation is adequate for
language study material.

## Mark as adequate when

- The translation conveys the meaning of the source phrase in the target language.
- A learner at the stated level would understand the sense of the phrase from the translation.
- The translation is not identical to the source phrase (unless the source is a proper loanword that stays unchanged in the target language and that is pedagogically acceptable).
- The translation is non-empty.
- The translation fits the sentence context.

## Mark as inadequate when

- The translation is empty or whitespace only.
- The translation is a lazy copy of the source phrase with no real translation work.
- The translation misses the core meaning of the phrase.
- The translation is clearly for a different word or sense than the source phrase in context.
- The translation is unusable for study (gibberish, wrong language, or unrelated content).

## Be pragmatic

- Idioms may use natural target-language equivalents rather than word-for-word renderings.
- Minor stylistic differences are acceptable if meaning is preserved.
- Focus on whether the translation would help a learner, not on literary perfection.
